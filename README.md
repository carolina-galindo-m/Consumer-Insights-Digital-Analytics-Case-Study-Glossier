# 🌸 Glossier Digital Marketing Analytics & Consumer Insights Case Study

This project is a full-funnel digital marketing analytics case study analyzing **Glossier’s** online performance across SEO, social media, consumer interest, and competitive dynamics. It was developed as part of a Marketing Analytics course and showcases skills in **consumer insights, trend analysis, data visualization, and strategic storytelling**—all essential in brand and marketing insights roles.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This case study evaluates Glossier’s brand performance using a blend of quantitative and qualitative insights. The analysis identifies:

- Key **search trends** shaping consumer demand  
- Global audience interest across markets  
- Social media **engagement behaviors & platform dynamics**  
- Seasonal momentum and product-level opportunities  
- Emerging trends influencing brand visibility  
- Strategic recommendations for brand growth  

The final deliverable is a visually structured slide deck that synthesizes data into clear, actionable insights for marketing and product teams.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

1. **Understand consumer behavior** through search patterns and rising queries  
2. **Analyze market-level interest** across countries and competitor brands  
3. **Evaluate social media performance** on Instagram and TikTok  
4. **Identify opportunities for brand growth** using SEO, content strategy, and trend analysis  
5. **Develop actionable recommendations** backed by insights  

---

## 🧠 Key Insights

### 🔍 Search & SEO
- Strong seasonal peaks during the holiday period  
- Fragrance dominates consumer interest  
- Opportunity to optimize for gifting, new product categories, and SEO-rich content  

### 🌍 Market Demand
- High demand in South Africa, India, and Australia  
- Low visibility in certain regions → opportunity for localized marketing  

### 📱 Social Media Insights
- TikTok = discovery engine (high virality)  
- Instagram = loyalty hub (steady engagement)  
- UGC and product‑forward content drive the highest reach and saves  

### 💡 Strategic Opportunities
- Strengthen seasonal SEO pages  
- Leverage fragrance dominance with optimized content  
- Expand globally through localized awareness strategies  
- Use TikTok wins to cross-amplify Instagram traffic
